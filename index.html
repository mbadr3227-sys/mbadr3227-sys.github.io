  1. ATS — Applicant Tracking System

    mbadr3227-sys/ats-app

    Recruiters post roles and move candidates through a pipeline.   7. Who is looking to leave

    mbadr3227-sys/ml-job-change-prediction

    Scoring 19,000 candidates on job-change intent. Where someone lives predicted it better than their experience or education — and the third strongest signal turned out to be a blank field. Missing employer data meant a 38.8% change rate against a 24.9% baseline, so it was kept as a category instead of filled in. 0.814 ROC-AUC.

    Python · scikit-learn · pandas · Jupyter

  8. HR attrition drivers

    mbadr3227-sys/hr-attrition-analysis

    Which employees leave, and what precedes it. Overtime tripled the attrition rate; sales roles ran sixteen times higher than research directors. Cleaned, encoded, and scaled 35 features into a modelling-ready set — and flagged why accuracy would be the wrong metric on a 16% minority class.

    Python · pandas · scikit-learn · Jupyter

    Data

  9. Gender Pay Gap Analysis

    mbadr3227-sys/gender-pay-gap-analysis

    An 8.6% raw pay gap that vanished under scrutiny. The t-test said the $8,515 difference was real (p < 0.001). The regression said it was about job titles and seniority, not gender — adjusted gap $858, p = 0.231. Performance ratings turned out to have no effect on pay at all.

    Python · pandas · SciPy · statsmodels · seaborn

    Data
